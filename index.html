How well do you know what chess players think?

Instructions

Before we begin, get your teams ready!

1

Get Into Teams

Get together with your friends and form your teams.

2

Work Together

Discuss possible answers with your teammates and think carefully.

Rules

Here's how Chess Family Feud works.

1

Listen to the Question

Each round begins with a chess-related question.

2

Guess the Answers

Work with your team to guess the answers hidden on the board. Top answer is worth 6 points, Second most popular answer is worth 5 points, etc.

3

Watch Your Strikes

Incorrect answers earn your team a strike. Three strikes gives the other team a chance to steal!

4

Have Fun!

Think like a chess player, work together, and enjoy the game.

♟️ Remember: The answers are based on the most common responses, so think like the average chess player!
